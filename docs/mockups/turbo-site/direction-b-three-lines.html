The macro you'd write yourself, made for every spell on your bars.

Type /turbo macros once, out of combat. Turbo writes these three lines for each attack spell, a plain cast macro for every other spell, and puts every macro where that spell sat on your bars.

Works for every class.

It does one thing, carefully.

There are no settings and no window. You run one command; Turbo changes only its own macros and the buttons where those spells sat.

What it does

  • Makes a character macro for every spell on your bars. Attack spells also start auto-attack.
  • Swaps each macro onto your action bars wherever that spell sat, at any rank.
  • Uses the spell names from your game, so the macros work in any game language.
  • Also answers to /tb macros.

What it leaves alone

  • Your own macros. Turbo never edits or deletes a macro it didn't make.
  • Anything during combat, or while your cursor is holding something. It tells you to try again.
  • Duplicates. Running it again finds its own macros and adds only what's missing.
  • Half-finished jobs. Without enough free character macro slots it changes nothing and tells you how many it needs.

Set up in a minute.

Four steps, and the last one is playing.

  1. Install

    Get Turbo from CurseForge.

  2. Log in

    Pick your character and enter the world.

  3. Type the command

    Out of combat, type /turbo macros.

  4. Play

    Your attack buttons are now macros, in the same slots.

New rank? Nothing to do. The macros carry no rank, so they already cast the newest one.

New attack spell? Put it on your bars and run /turbo macros again. It skips what's already done.
