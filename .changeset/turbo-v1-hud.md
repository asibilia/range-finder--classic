Turbo v1, the Enhancement Shaman HUD for WoW: Forever (#27–#36). Turbo loads only on Forever (interface 16001) and explains why on any other client; other classes get a one-time "Turbo doesn't support <Class> yet". Shamans get one compact HUD card that fades in while engaged, can be moved with Edit Mode and has an "always show" option. The card shows the range band (Melee, Shock range, Bolt range, Out of range), a main-hand swing timer with an adjustable safe window that greys during hard casts and dims out of range, four totem slots with countdown numbers, a swirl, a pulse near expiry and a red flash on early death, the weapon imbue with time left, Lightning Shield charges, the shared shock cooldown and Stormstrike, Maelstrom Weapon pips (off until readable in combat) and a mana bar that turns red under 20%. Silent, glowing reminders flag a missing or expiring imbue or Lightning Shield, even when the card is hidden, and stay quiet while resting, mounted or on a flight path. Settings live on a Turbo page in Options → AddOns and in `TurboDB`, split by class; `/turbo` and `/tb` print help and toggle modules and "always show", and `/turbo debug` logs which values are readable or secret. Turbo never reads a secret value and never changes the player's CVars or Blizzard frames.
