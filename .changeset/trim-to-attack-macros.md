Turbo is now just attack macros: `/turbo macros` (or `/tb macros`) makes a `/startattack` macro for each attack spell you know and puts it on your action bars. The Shaman HUD is gone (range finder, swing timer, totem timers, Recall reminder, weapon imbue and Lightning Shield reminders, key cooldowns, mana bar, Maelstrom Weapon, the settings page and `/turbo debug`), and Turbo no longer saves any data. Forever's own UI covers most of it: use the Cooldown Manager, Swing Timer, Personal Resource Display and Totem Frame instead. `docs/research/forever-native-ui.md` compares them with the old HUD.
